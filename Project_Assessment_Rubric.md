# Project Assessment Rubric

## Database Management Systems II

**Assessment Criteria & Evaluation Rubric**

| **Criterion** | **Marks** | **Assessment Focus** |
|----|:--:|----|
| 1\. ERD & Database Design | 10 | Complete ERD; ≥10 related tables; appropriate PK/FK; 1:1, 1:N and M:N relationships; clear cardinalities and business rules. |
| 2\. Normalization (3NF) | 5 | Correct normalization to 3NF; minimal redundancy; appropriate functional dependencies. |
| 3\. Oracle Database Implementation & Constraints | 15 | Correct table implementation; PK/FK, NOT NULL, UNIQUE, DEFAULT; ≥3 meaningful CHECK constraints; appropriate referential actions. |
| 4\. Sample Data & Testing | 5 | Sufficient realistic and consistent sample data; database constraints and functions properly tested. |
| 5\. SQL Queries & Advanced SQL | 15 | JOINs; GROUP BY/HAVING; subqueries; FROM subquery; Top-N with ROWNUM; ROLLUP/CUBE/GROUPING SETS + GROUPING(); required DML. |
| 6\. Views & Analytical Queries | 10 | ≥3 meaningful views; multi-table, aggregate/summary, and restricted/Top-N views; meaningful business analysis. |
| 7\. Transactions & Security | 5 | INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE; COMMIT/ROLLBACK; appropriate GRANT privileges and roles. |
| 8\. APEX Application Development | 15 | Working APEX application; dashboard/home page; Create, Update, Delete; navigation; forms; validation; database integration. |
| 9\. APEX Reports, Grids & Charts | 5 | ≥2 Interactive Reports; ≥2 Interactive Grids; ≥2 meaningful charts; at least one grid supports data manipulation. |
| 10\. Report & Documentation | 5 | Clear and professional report; ERD, normalization, SQL, views, APEX application, screenshots, testing, limitations, and documentation. |
| 11\. Demonstration & Viva | 10 | Successful project demonstration; clear explanation of implementation; ability of both members to answer questions about database, SQL, APEX, functionality, and testing. |
| **TOTAL** | **100** |  |

> **Important Note: Demonstration and Final Presentation**
>
> Students must successfully demonstrate their project and answer questions about their implementation during the final presentation. The final score will be based on their demonstrated understanding and performance; students who cannot explain or answer questions about their work will not receive marks for those components.

**Performance Interpretation Standard**

- Excellent: Complete, technically correct, meaningful, and well demonstrated.

- Good: Substantially complete with minor issues.

- Satisfactory: Core requirement is present but has noticeable omissions or weaknesses.

- Needs Improvement: Missing, substantially incomplete, or does not function as required.

## Extra Credit: APEX UI/UX & Aesthetic Design (+5 Maximum)

- Up to +5 marks for a particularly user-friendly, consistent, visually clear, and aesthetically polished APEX application.
- Consider navigation, layout, labels, page consistency, appropriate components/icons, readability, validation messages, and meaningful dashboard presentation.
- Extra credit does not replace missing core database, SQL, or application requirements.
